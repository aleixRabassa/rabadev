Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

RabaDev only accesses your YouTube and TikTok accounts to upload the videos you ask it to upload and to show you their statistics. Your credentials and data stay on your own computer. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising or sent to servers we operate.

Who we are

RabaDev is a personal tool operated by Aleix Rabassa (“we”, “us”), based in Catalonia, Spain. You can contact us at aleix.rabassa@gmail.com.

Information we access

When you connect your accounts and authorize the app, it can access:

  • From TikTok: your basic profile information (user ID, display name and avatar), the list of your videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments and shares), and the video files you choose to upload.
  • From YouTube: basic information about your channel, the videos you choose to upload with their metadata (title, description, tags, privacy status and thumbnail), and the statistics of your own videos.
  • On your computer: only the video files you explicitly select for upload.

We do not access your private messages, your followers' data, other users' content or your payment information.

How we use it

  • To upload the videos you request, with the settings you choose.
  • To show you the status and statistics of your own videos.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than the ones above.

Where it is stored

The app runs entirely on your own computer. Access tokens issued by YouTube and TikTok are stored in local files on your device. Video files are read from your disk and sent directly to YouTube or TikTok. We do not operate any server that receives, stores or processes your data.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Your videos and their metadata are sent only to the platform you choose (YouTube or TikTok), as needed to publish them.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube features of this app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke this app's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page.

TikTok

The TikTok features of this app use the TikTok Content Posting API and Display API, and are subject to TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. You can revoke this app's access at any time from the app permissions section of your TikTok account settings.

Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on your device until you revoke access or delete them. To delete all data held by the app, revoke access as described above and delete the app's local credential files. Because we do not store your data on any server, there is nothing further for us to delete; if you have any request, write to aleix.rabassa@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You can exercise these rights by writing to aleix.rabassa@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and we do not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
